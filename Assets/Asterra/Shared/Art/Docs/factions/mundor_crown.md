# Mundor Crown

**Keep:** royal citadel is a separate authored mesh (`building_royal_citadel` is skipped in the unique roster rebuild). Field buildings use limestone / plaster / slate.  
**Palette:** warm brick and plaster, slate roofs, iron hardware, wood doors, very little brass.

Mundane kingdom architecture: **recognisable medieval-fantasy civic buildings**. Barracks are long halls, the court is a portico, the farm is barn + silo + pen.

## Silhouette

- Horizontal halls and pitched slate, not wizard needles.
- Walls and turrets are coursed stone with merlons.
- Bridges are piers + timber deck, not magic spans.

## Units

Legion, guard, longbow, civic officers, spy line, onager, pathfinder, sapper. Shield-and-spear is the infantry read; do not hat-swap them.
