# University Guild

**Keep:** `building_university_grand_college`  
**Palette:** red brick and marble trim, slate, brass, glass, crystal used sparingly.

Wizard-engineers. Landmarks are **tall working machines in stone**: clock tower, observatory, far-glass pier, weather field. The college is a buttressed hall with a clock turret.

## Silhouette

- Observatory: octagon keep → tall shaft → hemisphere dome + real telescope.
- Clockwork: same masonry language, **clock faces and lantern**, not an orrery toy.
- Workshop is industrial sawtooth, library is stacks + dome, alchemist is chimneys and vats.

## Units

Gowned fellow and deans, mechanical spider, airship, trebuchet, earth-breaker. Engines must look engineered, not like furniture.
