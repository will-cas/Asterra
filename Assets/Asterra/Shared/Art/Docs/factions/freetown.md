# Freetown

**Keep:** `building_freetown_tavern`  
**Palette:** brick quay, jettied pale timber, slate, barrels, blue cloth/sails, iron hoops.

A **smuggler port**. The keep is a pub on a dock. Other buildings are piers, stalls, masts, and crate walls.

## Silhouette

- Ground storey stone, jetty timber above, steep slate.
- Water-side decks, piles, and bollards. The ferry dock *is* the building.
- Barricades are cargo, not curtain walls.

## Units

Drunk, crow, hound, crab, flamer, powder cart, boat, privateer, and the port’s named scoundrels. The boat and crab are vehicles/creatures, not reskinned infantry.
