# Uncrowned

**Keep:** `building_arcaneum`  
**Palette:** dark ashlar, iron/steel ribs, pale glass, ice-sapphire crystal, muted purple cloth on the keep only.

Heretical scholars and veiled hosts. Buildings should feel **occult and engineered**: layered dark stone, recessed bays, iron mullions, crystal as a structural jewel — not candy.

## Silhouette

- Keeps and halls read as **shafts, rings, and glass cages**, not timber cottages.
- Gates are standing rings or thorned portals, not castle barbicans.
- Towers taper; lanterns are glass with iron ribs.

## Units

Veiled humans, constructs (golem, colossus, elemental), stalkers, and named heretics. Staff/orb, thorns, and shadows must stay distinct from each other.
