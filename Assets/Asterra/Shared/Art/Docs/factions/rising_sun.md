# Rising Sun

**Keep:** `building_church_grand_temple`  
**Palette:** pale marble, gold used as sacred metal, wood doors, sun discs.

A **solar church**: portico, nave, drum and dome. Outbuildings are monastery cloister, rotunda, obelisk, shrine, scorched ruin, sacred walls.

## Silhouette

- Temple language is classical (columns, pediment, dome), not a gothic needle unless it is the scorched tower.
- Gold is discs, dome ribs, and emblems — not full gilding of the nave.
- Sacred site is a plaza monument, not a house.

## Units

Zealot, rider, radiant guard, solar engine, priests, inquisitor, reliquary. Gold is sacred metal on the kit, not a full gild.
