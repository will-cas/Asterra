# Outcast Host

**Keep:** `building_outcast_great_camp`  
**Palette:** bark and log, packed earth, ice caps, leather doors, green cloth on the keep.

A winter host living **in timber and mounds**, not masonry palaces. Snow and ice sit on roofs and palisade points.

## Silhouette

- Longhouses, burrow mounds, tree decks, mine headframes.
- Vertical language comes from trunks and palisade poles, not ashlar shafts.
- Village hall is a smaller sibling of the great camp, not a copy-paste keep.

## Units

Villagers, hobgoblin, hunters, beast rider, frost giant, cub, sprite, sky-eye, wold, and named host champions. Beasts are beasts, not men.
